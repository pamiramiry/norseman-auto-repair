Etobicoke Auto Repair Without the Guesswork

They quoted you for the part. Nobody checked if it was the part.

Some shops fix cars by swapping parts. A new battery. Still dead? A new alternator. Every guess goes on your bill, and the car still might not start on Monday morning.

Other places want $400 just to look at it. So you put it off. The check engine light stays on for another month, and you turn the radio up over the squeal.

When you finally bring it in, someone talks at you in part numbers and hands you a list. Nobody tells you which line actually matters.

What waiting costs you

A worn belt or tensioner can leave you stranded without warning. Brake pads left too long can damage the rotors. A tire that keeps losing air wears unevenly and can fail on the highway. A coolant leak can lead to overheating.

Usually, it's one part.

Most no-starts, noises and warning lights come down to a single part. Often it's a cheaper one than you were quoted. We'll tell you what it is before we touch it, and you'll know the price before you pay for anything.

Car Problems Etobicoke Drivers Bring to Our Mechanics

You don't need to know the name of the part. Tell us what the car is doing. Here's what these usually turn out to be.

Car won't start, or the battery keeps dying
Could be the battery, the alternator or a belt. Worth testing before anyone replaces anything. Call and describe it.
Squealing from the engine at start-up
Often a worn belt or tensioner. Call and describe it.
Check engine light on
It could be minor or it could not be. A scan tells us where to look. Call and describe it.
Squealing or grinding when you brake
Pads are often getting low. Grinding means book it now. Call and describe it.
Car pulls to one side, or the steering wheel shakes
Often alignment, tires or a worn suspension part. Call and describe it.
Tire pressure light won't go off
Usually a slow leak or a tire pressure sensor. Call and describe it.
A/C blowing warm
Often low refrigerant or a leak. Call and describe it.
Water on the floor inside the car
Usually a blocked drain or a seal. Call and describe it.

This is a guide, not a diagnosis. We'll know for sure once the car is in the bay.

Auto Repair in Etobicoke: Common Questions

How much does auto repair cost in Etobicoke?

It depends on the car and the part, so anyone who quotes you a number before looking is guessing. Here's how we work: you hear the price before any work starts. We find what's wrong, you hear what it is and what it costs, and the work only starts once you say yes.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Call ahead and we'll book you in. We fit people in on short notice when we can, but we can't guarantee it.

Do you charge to diagnose a problem?

Ask when you book. You'll know what the check costs before we look at the car, and we'll tell you what we find before any repair starts.

Can you fix a flat tire today?

Often, yes. Many punctures take about 10 minutes. Call first so we can tell you if there's room.

Do you sell new and used tires?

Yes, plus rims and tire pressure sensors.

Do you do body work?
